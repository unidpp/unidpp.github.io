== Negative conformance fixtures

[[annex-negative-intro]]
=== Purpose

This annex lists the negative test cases N1 to N6 of the European DPP
corpus defect register (<<unidpp-audit>>, Section F: conformance-run
findings against <<en18223>> Annex A, machine-verified 2026-09-07).
They are reproduced here as conformance test material: every fixture
is an instance of a class of defect that conforming implementations
(Clauses 12.2 and 12.5) shall reject or flag. The fixtures also serve
as evidence for the framework's counter-principles (5.2): tool-checked
content and registry-validated semantics over manually maintained
prose examples.

[[annex-negative-mapping]]
=== Defect classes

Each fixture instantiates one of the framework's seam failures
(5.10): N1 and N6 are serialization defects (core renders); N2 is a
seam S4 failure (profile to registry resolution); N3 is an
identity-uniqueness failure; N4 and N5 are normalization failures that
the framework's casing and separator normalization (7.1 e) and its
single canonical body rule (8.1) preclude at construction. The
underlying corpus-level lesson is documented in <<unidpp-audit>>: all
six normative annex examples of <<en18223>> fail machine validation,
while the profile adaptations (casing normalization, field-name
mapping) that reconcile the EU profile family with the neutral core
are exactly the profile mechanism of Clause 6 at work.
