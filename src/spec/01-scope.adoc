== Scope

This document specifies the UniDPP framework for digital product
passports. The framework defines a neutral core — one identity per
subject, one append-only event-sourced record per subject, typed
relationships between subjects, quantity-conserving transformations, and
a deterministic projection mechanism — together with the profile
mechanism by which jurisdiction, sector and characteristic requirements
constrain and transform, but never redefine, the single definition of
each data point.

This document is applicable to:

a) authors of profiles (jurisdiction regulators, sector bodies,
 characteristic-profile owners) and operators of the semantic registry
 in which profiles are registered;

b) implementers of passport cores, hosts and resolvers, including
 DPP service providers, national in-country hosts, manufacturer
 source systems and archive providers;

c) implementers of verifiers, including border and market-surveillance
 authorities, commercial verifiers, machines and auditors;

d) implementers of device edge segments (capability classes S2 and S3)
 that commit, reveal or serve portions of the event record.

This document specifies:

e) the design invariants of the framework (I1 to I14) and the seam
 register (S1 to S12) at which layers join;

f) the three-axial profile composition model, the profile manifest, the
 trigger-predicate language and the capability gating of profile
 requirements;

g) the identifier model, identifier granularity, identifier dormancy
 and the seven-class relationship algebra including the installation
 schema, recoverability spectrum and edge-visibility classes;

h) the typed event taxonomy, the append-only hash-chained event log,
 the status state machine and as-of query semantics;

i) the transformation algebra (split, combine, end-of-waste) with mass
 balance, taint propagation and unit binding;

j) the three payload tiers (Tier A carrier-embedded, Tier B served,
 Tier C archival), carrier budgets, freshness verdicts and the
 degradation ladder;

k) the graded trust model (trust markers, cryptographic suites,
 stamps, the three verification readings, coverage reports and
 retroactivity of revocation), in conformance with SIGNATIF;

l) the conformance classes (Core, Profile author, Host, Verifier,
 Device) and their requirements.

The following are outside the scope of this document:

m) the legal instruments, delegated acts and regulatory content that
 profiles bind — the framework standardizes the mechanism, and each
 regulator defines its profiles through the registry;

n) the content of individual data points, which is fixed by registered
 data-element definitions;

o) any single jurisdiction's serialization, addressing or transport
 requirements, which are profile bindings and not core requirements;

p) mappings to specific twin representations other than as informative
 annexes or registered transforms; in particular, any mapping to the
 Asset Administration Shell is informative at most and is never
 load-bearing.

Solutions presented in this document are realization-neutral: they
specify the optics (identity, events, projections, trust) and derive
representation formats, rather than legislating implementation details
of any single deployment.
