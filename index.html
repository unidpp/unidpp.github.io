An international framework and open reference implementation for the Digital Product Passport

UniDPP treats every regulatory regime — the European Union's EN 18216–18246 series among them — as one profile on a neutral international core: one product identity, one event-sourced record, many jurisdiction, sector, and characteristic lenses, with trust that crosses borders without requiring anyone to adopt anyone else's rules.

github.com/unidpp

The framework

Fourteen invariants define the framework: single-minted identity with registry-governed semantics; append-only event sourcing as the authoritative record; a typed relationship algebra covering association, derivation, installation, type lineage, custody, and membership; passports as deterministic, legally-cited projections of the testimonial record; graded trust with multi-suite signatures (SM2, FIPS, and post-quantum side by side); jurisdiction × sector × characteristic profiles with predicate triggers; enumeration resistance — the system is verifiable without being browsable; and a three-tier availability ladder with an offline minimum carried on the product itself.

Identity

ISO/IEC 15459 issuance with bridges to GS1, Handle, Ecode, and MA schemes; identities are never re-minted.

Semantics

ISO 19135 / FERIN federated registers; the UNTDED dataset as the trade-data seed; CDDAL dictionary technology.

Trust

SIGNATIF cross-recognition: threshold roots, jurisdictional trust lists, a multi-witnessed master list, transparency anchoring.

Time and events

ISO 8601 and ISO 34000 foundations; a fifteen-class post-sale event model; notarized as-of queries.

Units and measurement

ISO 80000 with UnitsDB/UnitsML; measurement uncertainty and conformity decision rules on every measured value.

Formal model

An EXPRESS core with derived JSON and XML renders; STEP/AP242 lineage for the type–version–configuration lattice.

Implementation

A fully open, multi-language implementation: unidpp-core (Rust — canonical model and algorithms: event log, transformation algebra with mass balance and taint, Tier-A packer, verdicts), unidpp-ts, unidpp-rb, unidpp-py (SDKs, registry clients, conformance runners), unidpp-resolver (federated resolution with dark identities), unidpp-signatif (trust path-finding and threshold ceremonies on Confium), and unidpp-explorer. Conformance fixtures are derived from the full converted corpus of the EN series, the CEN Workshop Agreements, and the IDTA specifications, including published defect registers as negative test cases.

Terminology

A multilingual Glossarist concept-browser edition of the Digital Product Passport vocabulary, compiled from the published European Standards, CEN Workshop Agreements, and ISO working drafts — including the recorded definitional conflict between ISO/AWI 25534-1 and EN 18223:2026, presented for harmonization under the proposed ISO 25534-1 Part 1 (Vocabulary).

Papers and contributions

Position and technical contributions to ISO/IEC JTC 5: the framework position paper; semantic-registry requirements (filling EN 18223 §4.3); the lifecycle and event model; the trust annex input; the terminology dataset announcement; and the UniDPP implementation report with published conformance and drift findings.
